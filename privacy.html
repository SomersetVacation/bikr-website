Privacy

Updated 25 October 2026. This page explains the privacy principles guiding Bikr during its private development phase.

Private beta notice: Bikr is not yet publicly released. This policy will be reviewed and expanded before public launch and should receive Australian legal review.

Our core principle

Bikr separates information about a motorcycle from personal information about a rider. Motorcycle records may be transferable when ownership changes. Personal rider information is not transferred by default.

Information Bikr may hold

Depending on the features you use, this may include account details, motorcycle identity and specifications, odometer readings, service and maintenance records, modifications, ownership events, uploaded documents and communications with support.

Bike-owned and rider-owned information

Bike-owned information may include VIN, model, kilometres, service history, modifications, workshop evidence and ownership events. Rider-owned information may include personal routes, private notes, preferences and personal ride activity. Bikr's product design keeps these categories separate.

How information may be used

Information may be used to provide and improve Bikr, maintain motorcycle records, issue maintenance reminders, support controlled ownership transfers, respond to enquiries, protect the service and comply with applicable law.

Sharing and transfers

Bikr does not sell personal information. A motorcycle passport transfer must be deliberately initiated and accepted. Account credentials and private rider information are not part of a bike transfer.

Storage and receipt scanning

Firebase provides sign-in and cloud motorcycle records. Photos, receipts and recorded rides are stored on your device by default. Receipt text recognition runs on your device; review suggestions before saving. Keep exports before changing phones or uninstalling. Pro cloud-file backup is not currently available.

Location and optional maps

When you start recording and grant permission, Bikr records your location, including with the screen locked when the phone permits it. Ride files remain local unless you export or share them. The optional MapTiler background sends map-area requests and ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, to MapTiler. These requests can reveal the area viewed. Bikr does not upload the full GPS trace to MapTiler for surface analysis.

Selected handover files

The seller explicitly selects records, photos and receipts for a buyer. Selected files are uploaded privately to Firebase; the accepted buyer can retrieve them while owning the motorcycle. Files are shared unchanged and may contain personal information, so review them first. Insurance documents, ride routes and unselected files are not automatically included. After acceptance the buyer controls the continuing record, while the seller retains a fixed sale snapshot.

Deletion and retained information

Deleting an account removes motorcycles still owned by that account, their records and private sale snapshots. Already-transferred records and files remain with the buyer. Current-device photo, receipt and ride folders are cleared by the new Development deletion flow; copies on other devices and external exports cannot be erased remotely. A minimal deletion identifier is retained to block old sessions. Automatic expiry cleanup of unused handover uploads is not yet enabled. Production retention periods remain to be finalised before launch.

Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information associated with a Bikr test account. See Delete account for the current process.
