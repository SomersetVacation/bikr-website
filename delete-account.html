ACCOUNT CONTROL

Delete your account

Private-beta testers can request deletion of their Bikr account and associated personal information without keeping the app installed.

Submit a request

Email getbikr.passport@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Bikr account. Use the subject “Delete my Bikr account”.

In the app

The new Development version provides Garage → Account → Delete account, with a recent password sign-in and explicit DELETE confirmation. This update is not yet in TestFlight; until it reaches your build, use the email request above.

Verification

We may ask you to verify control of the account before processing the request. Never send your password.

What deletion covers

Deletion removes your account, motorcycles you still own, their records and private sale snapshots. A buyer keeps the selected records and documents already transferred to them. A minimal identifier is retained to block old sessions. Export anything you want to keep first. External exports and copies on other devices cannot be erased remotely.

Subscriptions

Deleting an account does not cancel an Apple or Google subscription. Cancel any subscription through the store where you purchased it.

Timing

We will acknowledge the request and provide the expected completion timing. The production process and final retention periods will be documented before public launch.
