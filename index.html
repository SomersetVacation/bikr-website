BIKR FOR DEALERS

Turn used inventory into trusted inventory.

For motorcycle stores managing dozens of used bikes, Bikr becomes the fast visual workspace for intake, inspection, reconditioning, evidence and customer handover—on iPad or the web.

  • Scan a VIN or Bikr QR to begin intake
  • Track inspection, workshop work and preparation costs
  • Publish a buyer-friendly history without exposing private data
  • Transfer the passport at delivery with a complete audit trail

OWNERSHIP, WITHOUT THE PAPER CHASE

A safer handover in four clear steps.

  1. 01

    Seller starts

    The current owner selects the bike and chooses exactly what belongs in its passport.

  2. 02

    Buyer confirms

    The buyer accepts through a secure invitation—never by sharing passwords.

  3. 03

    Bikr records

    The ownership event is added to the bike timeline with a clear audit trail.

  4. 04

    Private stays private

    Personal routes, notes, preferences and rider history remain with the seller.
